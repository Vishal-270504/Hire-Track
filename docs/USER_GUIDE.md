# HireTrack user guide

This guide is for the people who use the app every day: HR, recruiters, interviewers, IT and admins. It explains every screen, why it is there, and the steps for each thing you can do. You do not need to know any code.

To sign in, use the work email you were given. For a test run, use the demo accounts on the sign-in page (open "Try a demo role", then click a name). The demo password is `hiretrack-demo`.

---

## 1. Signing in

**Why it is there:** so only the right people see candidate and salary information.

1. Type your work email and password, then press **Sign in**.
2. If you forget your password, ask an admin to set a new one for you.
3. To change your own password: click your name at the bottom left, choose **Change password**, enter your current password and a new one (at least 10 characters). You are then signed out and sign in again with the new password.

The sign-in page has an optional **Try a demo role** section. It is collapsed by default. Click it to open the list, then click a name to fill in that account. It is for trying the app only.

---

## 2. The main menu (left side)

Each item appears only if your role is allowed to use it.

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Your to-do list. Start here every day. |
| **Candidates** | Every person in the hiring process, in one table. |
| **Board** | The same people, shown as columns by stage. |
| **Probation** | People who have joined and are on probation. |
| **Reports** | Numbers about hiring, offers and joining. |
| **Roles** | Every job you are hiring for. |
| **Settings** | The rules and wording the process uses. |
| **Team** | The people who sign in, and what they can do (admins only). |

At the top right is the **bell**. It shows how many reminders are waiting for you. Click it to go to Today.

At the bottom left is your name. Click it for: change password, appearance (match my computer, light or dark), and sign out.

---

## 3. Today

**Why it is there:** so you never have to hunt for what needs doing.

- Reminders are grouped as **Overdue**, **Today** and **Coming up**.
- Each reminder says what to do, which person it is about, and has a link to open them.

**Steps**

1. Read the list from the top. Overdue items come first.
2. To open the person, click **Open** under the reminder.
3. To copy a ready-made message (for example an interview invitation), click **Copy text**, then paste it into your email or WhatsApp.
4. When you have done the thing, click **Done** to remove the reminder.

If you are an HR or recruiter user, you also see key numbers at the top: open roles, offers pending, interviews today, people who joined this month, days to hire, and offer acceptance.

---

## 4. Roles (jobs you are hiring for)

**Why it is there:** a role must be approved before anyone can apply. The approval checks the budget and the number of people needed.

### The list
- Use the tabs at the top to filter: waiting for approval, open, on hold, drafts, not approved, closed.
- Click a role to open it.

### Creating a role (the four steps)
1. **Click "New role".**
2. **The role:** job title, level (for example SDE-2), how many people, team, location, whether remote is allowed, the most you can pay (in lakhs per year), and the target joining date. Click **Save and continue**.
3. **Skills and experience:** add the must-have skills (type a skill, press Enter), the nice-to-have skills, the experience range, the longest notice period you accept, and a job description. Click **Save and continue**.
4. **Hiring plan:** choose a starting plan (the standard five rounds, a lean three-round plan, or a senior plan that starts with a take-home). You can rename rounds, change their order, change the pass marks, and add or remove rounds. Click **Save hiring plan**, then **Continue to review**.
5. **Review and send:** check the summary, choose who approves and in what order, then click **Send for approval**.

Each step saves as you go, so you can close the page and come back.

### Opening a role
- **Details:** the facts about the role.
- **Hiring plan:** the rounds, and the editor if the role is still open to changes.
- **Approvals:** who has approved, who is still to decide, and any reasons given.
- **History:** every change, who made it and when.

### What the buttons do
- **Send for approval / Send again:** starts the approval chain. Use **Send again** after a role was not approved and you have fixed the reason.
- **Approve / Not approve:** shown to the approver whose turn it is. "Not approve" needs a short reason, and the role goes back to the recruiter.
- **Edit details:** only while the role is a draft or not approved.
- **Publish to careers page:** shows the role on the public careers page so people can apply. Only open roles can be published.
- **Put on hold:** pauses the role and puts its candidates on hold. Give a reason. **Reopen** releases them.
- **Close role:** ends the search. Give a reason.
- **More → Delete draft:** removes a draft that was never sent.

## 5. Candidates

**Why it is there:** the one place to find anyone in the process.

### Finding someone
- Type in the search box: a name, email, phone number, PAN, or role.
- Use the filters: stage, role, and on hold or active.
- Use **Ready-made or saved views** (the dropdown next to the search) for lists like "Feedback overdue" or "Offers awaiting reply". Those are built for you.
- Click a name to open the full record.

### Saving a view
1. Set the filters you want.
2. Click **Save this view**, give it a name, and tick **Share with the team** if others should see it.

### Export
- Click **Export to Excel** to download the list as a spreadsheet, using the filters you have set.

### Adding a candidate
1. Click **Add candidate**.
2. Choose the open role they applied for, and enter their name and a way to reach them (email or 10-digit phone).
3. Choose where they came from.
4. Upload their resume (PDF, Word or text), or paste the text. The score is worked out from it.
5. Fill in what you know: experience, notice period, expected salary, work permit, shift, and links.
6. Click **Save candidate**.

If the person already exists, the app shows who it looks like and asks whether to add a new application. Choose **Add as a new application** only if it really is a second application.

## 6. A candidate's record

The top of the record shows their name, role, stage and status, and **one main button**. That button is always the next step. Examples: **Hold the phone screen**, **Start interviews**, **Schedule Technical interview 1**, **Make or record an offer**, **Joining checklist**.

The **More** menu has the less common actions: add a note, put on hold, move to another role, change owner, keep in talent pool, they withdrew, reject, and (for HR and admins, when closed) remove personal details.

### Overview tab
- **Screening score:** out of 100, with a bar for each part. Shows the skills found and missing.
- **Things to check:** flags such as salary above budget, notice period too long, or job gaps.
- **Contact and resume:** edit details and the resume text. Saving scores the resume again.
- **Knock-out questions:** ask these on the phone screen, then record the answers here: expected salary, notice period, work permit, shift, and where they live.

### Phone screen (main button at the start)
1. Click **Hold the phone screen**.
2. Choose whether it went well or not, and write a few notes.
3. Click **Save the call**.

If the notice period is longer than allowed, a manager must approve first. Use **Approve long notice period**.

### Start interviews
After a good phone screen, click **Start interviews**. The rounds from the role's hiring plan are created for this person.

### Interviews tab
Each round shows its status, when it is, who is on the panel, and any feedback given.

- **Schedule / Reschedule:** pick the date and time, add the meeting or test link, and choose the interviewers. Free interviewers are suggested. The time is shown in your own time zone. A round can be moved twice; a third time needs HR approval.
- **Calendar invite:** downloads a file you can open in any calendar app.
- **Did not turn up:** records a no-show. The first one allows a rebook; a second needs HR to confirm the rejection.
- **Add my feedback (interviewers):** write a few sentences and score each skill from 1 to 5, or choose hire or do not hire. Feedback is needed before a decision.
- **Enter test score (online test):** type the percentage from the test report.
- **I know this candidate:** declare a conflict of interest. HR then picks someone else.
- **Change interviewers (HR):** replace people on the panel.
- **Decide:** when the panel disagrees, or a round was not passed, HR decides to move them on or reject them, with a note.
- **Review the concern:** when a round is on hold for an integrity concern, HR decides and writes what was found.
- **Send the test again:** allowed once, with a reason.
- **Final approval (HR):** after the last round. Approving makes the person selected and ready for an offer.

Interviewers only see the candidates they are on the panel for, and only see other people's feedback after everyone on the panel has given theirs.

### Offer tab (HR and admins only)
- **Make an offer:** enter the salary (lakhs per year), the joining date, and optionally a fixed part. Within budget, it is sent at once. Above budget, it waits for an approver.
- **Approve and send:** an approver's button for offers above budget.
- **Record a yes (signed):** type the candidate's full name as their signature. The app records the date and time.
- **Record a counter-request:** they asked for something different. Write what they asked for.
- **Record a no:** write why they declined. The app then suggests other candidates for the same role.
- **Revise the offer:** send a new version. Earlier versions are kept.
- **Extend the reply date:** adds time, once or up to the limit set in Settings.
- **Revoke the offer:** for example after a background-check finding. Give a reason.
- **They backed out:** after they signed, if they change their mind. Their joining tasks are cancelled.

Candidates can do most of this themselves on their own status page (see section 12).

### Joining tab (HR and admins for documents and checks)
- **Documents:** the list of papers to collect (PAN, Aadhaar, photo, marksheets, and so on). For each one you can upload the file, mark it checked, ask for a replacement with a reason, accept an affidavit, or mark it "to follow" with a note.
- **Background checks:** set each check (identity, education, employment, address, criminal record, references) to in progress, clear, or flagged. A flag puts the person on hold for the HR head to decide.
- **Prep tasks:** jobs for HR, IT and admin before the joining day (create the employee ID, order the laptop, request access, order the ID card, and so on). The team that owns a task clicks **Mark done**.
- **Buddy and manager:** type who will help them settle in, and who they report to.
- **Check-ins:** a short note each time you speak to them during their notice period. The app reminds you every two weeks.
- **Change the joining date:** allowed once. Open tasks move with it.
- **Relieving letter:** mark received, or "it will follow" (you are reminded in 30 days).
- **Day 1: join:** creates the employee record, the employee code, and their company email. Tick "join subject to background checks" if checks are still pending; their access then stays restricted.
- **No-show on Day 1:** closes the record and cancels the joining tasks.

### Onboarding and probation tab (after joining)
- **Onboarding checklist:** each item has an owner (HR, IT or admin). The owner ticks it when done. Once every access item is ticked, probation starts automatically.
- **Assets:** record the laptop with its serial number (or mark a loaner), ID card, headset, and access card. Mark items returned when they come back.
- **Probation reviews:** at 30, 60 and 90 days, and a final review. Choose a score from 1 to 5 for each criterion. The app works out the weighted score and the outcome. Someone other than the person who wrote a review must approve it.
- **Start an improvement plan:** for someone who is not meeting expectations. Give 1 to 3 months and the goals. Record a weekly check-in, then mark the goals met or not met.
- **Confirm employment:** once the final review is approved and the score is high enough. The confirmation letter becomes available.
- **Extend probation once:** only for a final score in the middle range, with a plan.
- **Separate during probation:** HR must check the reviews were on time and a plan was given (or record serious misconduct). The HR head (an admin) must approve. Offboarding tasks are created.
- **Record a survey:** at day 30 and day 90, the rating and comments.
- **Confirmation letter / Termination letter:** shows the letter text to copy.

### Timeline tab
- **What happened:** every step in order, who did it, and when.
- **Record of changes:** the audit trail of who changed what.

## 7. Board

**Why it is there:** to see where everyone is at a glance, and to move people along where that is allowed.

- Each column is a stage: Screening, Interviews, Offer, Pre-joining, Joined (onboarding), Probation, Confirmed, Closed.
- Each card shows the person, role, score, status and next step.
- Click the name to open the full record.
- Click **Move to** on a card. Steps that can be done here are enabled. Steps that need a reason, or need a different screen, are greyed out with the reason shown. For example, "Pass the phone screen first".

---

## 8. Probation

**Why it is there:** so no review is missed and nobody is confirmed or let go by accident.

- One row per person on probation.
- **Days left**, the **next review** and its date (in red if late, amber if due within a week), the **latest score**, the trend of scores, and the status (on track, extended, needs improvement, onboarding).
- Click a name to open the record and record the review.

---

## 9. Reports

**Why it is there:** to see how hiring is going, without building a spreadsheet yourself.

- **Key numbers:** open roles, openings, offers pending, interviews today, joined this month, average days to hire, offer acceptance, probation pass rate, people leaving within 90 days, and days to final review. Hover over a number to see how it is worked out.
- **Where candidates are:** a bar for each stage.
- **Why people did not join or stayed:** withdrawals, declined offers, no-shows and so on.
- **Export candidates (Excel):** downloads the list.
- **Export audit log:** admins only. Downloads every recorded change.

---

## 10. Team (admins only)

**Why it is there:** to decide who can sign in and what they can do.

- **Add a person:** name, work email, role and a starting password. Share the password in person.
- **Change a role:** use the dropdown next to the person.
- **Turn access off / on:** a person whose access is off cannot sign in. Their history stays in place.

Roles:
- **HR:** runs hiring, offers, documents, joining and probation decisions. Sees salary.
- **Recruiter:** adds candidates, screens, schedules interviews.
- **Interviewer:** sees only their own candidates and gives feedback.
- **IT:** completes IT tasks and onboarding items.
- **Admin:** everything, plus team, settings and the HR head's approval of separations.

---

## 11. Settings (HR and admins can change; everyone can read)

**Why it is there:** every number and sentence the process uses lives here, so you can change it without asking a developer.

Each area has its own tab:
- **Screening:** how resumes are scored (five weights that must add up to 100), and the score for shortlisting and for manual review.
- **Interviews:** pass marks, how many times a round can be moved, feedback deadlines, and reminder times.
- **Offers and joining:** how long an offer stays open, how many times it can be extended, the notice-period limit, joining rules, and how long before someone who was rejected can apply again.
- **Probation:** length, the criteria and their weights (must add up to 100), and the score marks for confirming and for an improvement plan.
- **Checklists:** the documents to collect, the access items, the leaving checklist, and the background checks.
- **Messages:** the text for invitations, reminders, rejections, letters, and the company name. Words in curly brackets, such as {name}, are filled in automatically.

**Steps to change a setting:** change the number or the text, then click **Save**. The Save button is only active when something has changed. If something is wrong (for example the weights do not add up to 100), the message tells you. To go back to the original values, click **Reset to plan**.

---

## 12. The candidate's own page (for candidates)

Candidates do not sign in. They use the private link that the careers page gave them, which looks like `/status/...`.

- They see where their application is, in plain words.
- They upload documents when asked. A file can be a PDF, PNG, JPG or text, up to 5 MB.
- When an offer is open, they can type their full name to accept it, ask to discuss it, or decline.

---

## 13. The careers page (for the public)

- `/careers` lists the open roles that have been published, with their main skills and location. The budget is never shown.
- Click a role, fill in the form, tick the consent box, and send. Keep the link you are shown: it is the only way back to the application.

---

## 14. Quick reference: who does what

| Task | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Approve a role | The person named as the approver for that step |
| Add a candidate, screen, schedule interviews | Recruiter, HR, admin |
| Give interview feedback | The interviewer on that panel |
| Make an offer, record the reply, join, documents, background checks | HR, admin |
| Approve an offer above budget | HR, admin |
| Approve a probation review, confirm, extend, separate | HR (separation also needs the admin) |
| Complete IT tasks and laptop items | IT |
| Change settings | HR, admin |
| Manage the team | Admin |
| Remove a closed candidate's personal details | HR, admin |

## 15. If something looks wrong

- A button is greyed out: the message beside it says why.
- A message says "not allowed for your role": you do not have access to that step. Ask HR or an admin.
- Something did not save: read the message in red next to the field, fix it, and try again.
